# Combat Resolution Contract

**Status:** partially confirmed; formulas and several edge cases remain proposed/unresolved.

## Combat model
The established behavior implies a timed, simultaneous simulation rather than conventional alternating tabletop turns: Basic Attack timers are independent, same-timestamp Basic Attacks resolve simultaneously, and Ultimates pause Basic Attack timers. Whether this should be treated as real-time tick simulation is a recommended interpretation, not a fully approved global model.

## Initiative and opening
1. At battle start, every Champion rolls `d20 + Initiative Modifier`.
2. The Champion(s) with the highest result receive an opening bonus Ultimate.
3. The bottom half of Champions by initiative roll skip their first Basic Attack.
4. Basic Attack timers are independent of initiative.
5. Median-boundary tie behavior for deciding the bottom half is unresolved.

## Basic attacks and accuracy
- Standard Champions: natural 1 always misses; natural 20 always hits and critically hits.
- Rogue: critical range is natural 16–20.
- Sorcerer: natural 1–2 always miss; natural 19–20 are critical only if the attack otherwise hits; natural 20 always hits and critically hits.
- Proposed attack roll: `d20 + Accuracy` against `AC = 10 + Evasion + AC bonuses`. This formula is PROPOSED, not confirmed.
- Unmodified d20 determines critical range.
- Rogue resource is granted once per qualifying critical attack, not once per damage instance.
- Sorcerer resource is generated by its own qualifying attack criticals and misses.

## Damage and defenses
- Physical damage is mitigated by Armor; proposed formula: `damage * 100 / (100 + max(0, Armor))`.
- Magic Resistance mitigation and whether it applies to every magical type remain unresolved.
- Defense layers include AC/avoidance, Armor, Magic Resistance/type resistance, saving throws, immunity/vulnerability, shields and temporary damage reduction.
- Exact resolution order is unresolved and must be specified before implementing all effects.
- Natural 20 standard attacks always hit and critically hit; exact crit multiplier is type/class-specific and partly unresolved.

## Resource and Ultimate rules
- Class Resource is generated by class-specific events.
- Example: Barbarian gains Rage whenever it takes damage.
- When resource reaches its target, the Ultimate triggers immediately; resource resets to zero with no excess carry-over.
- Opening bonus Ultimate is separate from resource-triggered Ultimates.
- Ultimates have priority over Basic Attacks.
- Basic Attack timers pause during Ultimate activation/resolution and resume with progress preserved.
- If multiple Ultimates become ready simultaneously, queue by initiative descending.
- Before each queued Ultimate resolves, recheck caster and target validity.
- Equal-initiative tie-break: deterministic rule required; Champion instance ID then board slot is a proposal.
- Intended average is about 1–3 normal Ultimate activations per Champion per battle, excluding the opening bonus.

## Damage types
The 13 types are Bludgeoning, Piercing, Slashing, Acid, Cold, Fire, Force, Lightning, Thunder, Necrotic, Poison, Psychic and Radiant. Each type should have mechanical identity. Example concepts include Acid reducing Armor, Cold slowing/freezing, Fire burning/spreading, Lightning chaining/conducting, and Thunder staggering/interruption. These are design examples; exact implementation belongs to approved per-type rules and explicit ability definitions. A damage type alone does not automatically apply a status unless the ability explicitly says so.

## Overtime
If a battle lasts more than 30 seconds, damage dealt by all units increases exponentially until a winner emerges. The escalation formula, cadence and hard maximum are unresolved.

## Combat event requirements
The engine should emit typed, ordered events for at least: battle start, initiative roll, attack scheduled, attack roll, hit/miss, critical, damage, mitigation, shield change, effect applied/removed, resource change, Ultimate queued/started/resolved, Champion defeated, death-save event if implemented, overtime escalation, battle end and draw. The precise event list should be refined with the engine slice.
