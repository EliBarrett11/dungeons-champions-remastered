# Dungeon Routes, Monster Rounds and Rewards

## Route choices
At the start of each Floor, players choose among routes that reveal their rewards, risks and challenges. Route effects persist through the Floor and can alter rewards, PvP modifiers, PvE opponents and difficulty. Routes converge at the next Floor.

Floor 1 has three Monster Rounds before the first PvP Round. The exact later-Floor schedule is unresolved.

## Monster rewards
- Winning a Monster Round presents three reward bundles; the player chooses one.
- Each bundle contains an exact Gold amount, Item Components and actual Champions.
- Bundles should have equal total value but different composition.
- Rewards are mostly random with mild adaptive influence from the player's team, Traits, Items, Gold and Renown.
- Rewards are not guaranteed to be useful to the current build.
- Fairness goal: equalize total reward value/count over time, potentially through a per-player reward budget.

## Monster difficulty
Ordinary Monster Rounds should be weaker than the team of the current last-placed player. An odd-player PvE replacement opponent is a different case and should match the average team strength of remaining players.

## Unresolved
Exact route count, route-generation algorithm, Floor schedule, Monster roster, reward-value accounting, bundle generation, adaptive weights, and boss/Dragon encounter mechanics.
