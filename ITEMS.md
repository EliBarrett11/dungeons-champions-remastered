# Items and Components

## Confirmed rules
- There are exactly nine Item Components.
- Each Component is focused on one Role.
- Components have no shared-pool supply limit.
- Every pair of Components produces a unique completed Item: 45 recipes total (36 different pairs plus 9 identical pairs).
- When a second Component is equipped onto a Champion who already has an eligible Component, the pair automatically combines into a completed Item.
- Each Champion can hold at most three completed Items.
- A completed Item cannot be unequipped while its Champion remains owned.
- Completed Items cannot be dismantled.
- Selling a Champion returns its completed Items to the player's inventory, not to Component form.
- When a player is eliminated, all of that player's items and components are destroyed rather than transferred.

## Unresolved implementation details
- Names and effects of all nine Components and 45 completed Items.
- Exact temporary Component slot/equip UX and handling of invalid pairs.
- Item stat stacking, eligibility restrictions, unique-item rules and effects.
- Whether components can be moved between Champions before combination.
